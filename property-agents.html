Questions

Before you message us

What do I need to send?

For each listing: type (for example 2 BHK), locality, price, size, furnishing and any extras you want mentioned. Photos are welcome but not required for the copy.

Will you make up details?

No. We only use the facts you send. If something isn't provided, it isn't mentioned.

When do I pay?

After you've seen and approved the first listing. Payment is by UPI.

Can I see a sample of my own listing first?

Yes. Send one listing on WhatsApp and we'll send back the pack. That's the first of the ten.
