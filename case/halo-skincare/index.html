Halo Skincare

A soft, confident identity and packaging for a skincare line.

Concept project for a fictional brand, made by Vividoro to show our range. Photography from Unsplash contributors.

The brief

A new skincare label wants an identity that feels gentle but credible: a logo, a colour system and packaging that look good on a shelf and on a screen.

Our approach

A high-contrast serif wordmark with a ring mark gives the brand a memorable signature. A rosewood-to-blush palette keeps everything warm, and simple labels let the amber glass bottles carry the packaging.

What we made

  • Logo and ring mark
  • Colour palette and type specimen
  • Packaging labels (serum, cleanser)
  • Brand pattern
