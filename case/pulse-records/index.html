Pulse Records

A bold website and music player for an independent record label.

TypeWebsite · Music
Our roleStrategy · Web design · Player UI
TimelineConcept, about 5 days
StatusConcept for a fictional brand

Concept project for a fictional brand, made by Vividoro to show our range. Photography from Unsplash contributors.

Scope

What's in the project

A colour-graded turntable photo and a huge gradient wordmark set the mood. Release cards give each record its own artwork, and a persistent player bar means visitors can keep listening while they browse.

Identity

The logo and how it flexes

The primary logo

Five bars that are both a waveform and a pulse. The magenta to violet gradient is the label's signature and only appears on the mark.

Logo system

The lockup adapts to light, dark and brand-colour surfaces, and the mark stands alone as an app icon, avatar and favicon.

System

Colour, type and voice

Brand board

Four colours, one headline typeface (Bebas Neue) paired with Inter for body text, a repeatable pattern from the mark, and three simple rules for voice, spacing and colour.

Homepage

The main screen: one clear action above the fold.

Mobile

The full homepage

Below the hero: the reasons to choose the brand, a feature gallery, the key actions and a footer with contact details. Scroll the frame to see the whole page.

Brand in use

Beyond the screen

Stationery

Simple black and lilac stationery. The gradient mark gives it a spark without any extra decoration.

Social media

Release announcements, gig posters and demo calls. Photography is colour-graded to the palette so the feed has one voice.

Print and merchandise

Gig posters, tote bags and a backstage pass. The waveform mark prints well on vinyl labels and tees.

The brief

An independent label needs a home on the web that sounds like its music: loud, a little strange, and built around listening.

The challenge

An independent label needs a home that sounds like its music: loud, a little strange and built for listening, not reading.

Our solution

A colour-graded turntable hero under a huge gradient wordmark, release cards with their own artwork and a persistent player so the music never stops while visitors browse.

Thinking

Key design decisions

Make the type the poster

A giant condensed wordmark carries the label's personality before any image loads.

Artwork first

Each release gets its own colour world, so the grid feels like a record shelf.

A player that stays

A fixed player bar means browsing never interrupts listening.
