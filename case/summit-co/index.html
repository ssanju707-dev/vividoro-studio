Summit Co.

An outdoor-gear storefront and social posts built around the trail.

Concept project for a fictional brand, made by Vividoro to show our range. Photography from Unsplash contributors.

The brief

An outdoor brand needs a shop that sells the adventure first and the product second, with social posts that match.

Our approach

The hero puts the product in its natural setting, with a clear season call to action. A tidy new-arrivals row keeps shopping simple, and the social template reuses the same photography and orange accent so the brand feels consistent.

What we made

  • Storefront homepage
  • New-arrivals product row
  • Social post template
  • Orange accent system
