Orbit Fitness

A high-energy website and app screen for a 30-day fitness challenge.

Concept project for a fictional brand, made by Vividoro to show our range. Photography from Unsplash contributors.

The brief

A fitness brand wants people to start a 30-day challenge and keep going. The site and app need to feel energetic and make booking a class effortless.

Our approach

A dark interface with a single lime accent keeps focus on the call to action. Big condensed headlines, a member spotlight and a class schedule create momentum, and the app screen turns progress into a streak ring.

What we made

  • Homepage with challenge sign-up
  • Today's classes with booking
  • Member spotlight
  • App progress screen
