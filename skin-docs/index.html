About Dr Kasparis

Patient satisfaction comes first.

Dr Christos Kasparis is a UK-trained consultant dermatologist, practising in dermatology since 2008. He trained at Manchester University, then in general medicine, and has worked in respected dermatology centres across the UK.

He treats adults and children with all types of skin condition, with particular expertise in skin surgery, from skin tags, moles and cysts to skin cancer removal and repair, plus some aesthetic procedures. He also trains medical students and junior doctors, and has been a core member of a Skin Cancer Multidisciplinary Team.

Questions

Before you book.

Do you offer video consultations?

Yes. Video consultations are available as a 20-minute appointment.

Is the aesthetic consultation really free?

Yes. Aesthetic consultations are 30 minutes, free of charge, and include a tailored treatment plan covering injectable therapies such as Botox and fillers.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Dr Kasparis treats both adults and children with all types of skin condition.
